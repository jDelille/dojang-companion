PARTIAL PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS / CONTRACT GAPS
4. Overview now summarizes the supplied facts and the setup checklist. It does
   not manufacture retention/engagement scores or a full promotion-readiness
   percentage from attendance alone.
5. Quick/AI edit mockups call for preferred name, program, primary instructor
   and tags; training also references team/home location. update_member permits
   name/email/phone. Companion's legacy Program selector maps to course-roster
   assignment; this app exposes that supported action explicitly as course-roster
   management instead of assuming the first course is a canonical program.
   Other assignment fields are not returned/writable in the inspected Hub API.
6. Attendance percentage, lifetime class count and join date are not supplied by
   the current member_details contract. Do not derive exact lifetime statistics
   from a limited attendance window or invent a percentage denominator.
7. Complete progression readiness in Atlas p. 19 / broad writeup p. 17 includes
   time in rank, curriculum/evaluations, instructor assessment and account state.
   Attendance-threshold eligibility alone is not full promotion readiness.
8. Family workspace design distinguishes communicator, payer, booking, pickup and
   relationship-edit authority. Current household/channel/consent/PIN reads do not
   establish all of those grants. Family messaging/booking is not a bulk command.
9. General member-note read/write, clinical-field editing, and authorized file
   upload/download are not exposed by the inspected Hub contracts. Native Odoo
   capabilities must be exposed through approved scoped adapters before wiring.
10. Complete communication/activity history, automated retention decisions, and
    member-specific AI action proposals remain broader product work. Current
    message/timeline windows and contextual questions do not fulfill all of it.
11. Lead → Trial → Enrolled is not a completed membership lifecycle: the existing
    trial conversion workflow remains separate from Member 360 plan reads.
